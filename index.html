      Frequently asked questions

      Everything you need to know before partnering with FareRadar.

      How does FareRadar find low fares?

      We aggregate fares from airline APIs, GDS feeds, and OTA partners. Every time a traveler searches we recheck partner availability in real time, apply the latest offers, and show the refreshed price in GBP alongside our transparent service fee.

      Do travelers book on FareRadar?

      Yes. We offer on-site booking so travelers complete checkout without being redirected. Partners receive confirmed reservations via API or email.

      How is commission handled?

      FareRadar retains an agreed percentage of the ticket value which is itemised as our service fee during checkout. We settle commissions with partners on a monthly basis with detailed reporting.

      Can we integrate ancillary products?

      Absolutely. Seat selection, baggage, insurance and hotel upsells can all be merchandised during checkout.
